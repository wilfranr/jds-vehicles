## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the precision, trust, and luxury of an executive automotive showroom adapted for digital-first retail. Designed for discerning car buyers, collectors, and modern vehicle seekers, the aesthetic balances high-end refinement with effortless transactional clarity.

### Aesthetic Foundation
- **Modern Executive Precision:** A marriage of deep structural tones with razor-sharp editorial balance. Whitespace is generous and intentional, treating every vehicle showcase like an architectural centerpiece.
- **Dynamic Tactility:** Subtle luminous accents evoke automotive dashboard instrumentation, laser headlamps, and polished metallic trim without veering into playful skeuomorphism.
- **Trust & Assurance:** Information architecture prioritizes transparent specifications, certified vehicle statuses, and instant concierge-grade communication channels.

## Colors

The palette establishes an authoritative, high-end showroom atmosphere with deliberate color mapping:

- **Primary (`#0F172A` - Deep Obsidian Slate):** Anchors structural architecture, high-emphasis text, primary headers, and prominent vehicle titles. Provides grounding contrast and timeless prestige.
- **Secondary (`#D97706` - Warm Automotive Amber):** Drives key transactional actions, featured badges, certified premium highlights, and financing calls-to-action. Reflects precision engineering and warm automotive luxury.
- **Tertiary (`#06B6D4` - Electric Cyan):** Used for electric vehicle (EV) indicators, high-tech performance specs, active filter highlights, and battery/efficiency indicators.
- **Neutral (`#64748B` - Cool Slate Gray):** Standardizes secondary labels, inactive borders, body copy, and metadata icons.

### Contextual Accents & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base:** `#F8FAFC` provides an ultra-clean, museum-like floor that prevents eye fatigue.
- **Card Surface:** Crisp white `#FFFFFF` layered over the canvas for distinct, crisp contrast.
- **Concierge Accent (WhatsApp):** `#25D366` is strictly reserved for the direct dealer concierge CTA, paired with `#FFFFFF` icons to maintain instantaneous recognizable utility.
- **Status Semantic Palette:**
  - *Nuevo:* Emerald tint (`#059669` text on `#ECFDF5` background).
  - *Seminuevo:* Slate Cyan (`#0891B2` text on `#ECFEFF` background).
  - *Reservado:* Amber Gold (`#D97706` text on `#FFFBEB` background).
  - *Vendido:* Neutral Slate (`#64748B` text on `#F1F5F9` background).
  - *Próximamente:* Violet tint (`#7C3AED` text on `#F5F3FF` background).

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans delivers structural legibility, contemporary geometry, and subtle editorial finesse across digital surfaces.

- **Headlines:** Use tightened negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) on large headlines to produce an athletic, sculpted posture resembling luxury marque typography.
- **Specifications & Pricing:** Tabular numbers and medium-to-bold weights are employed for technical metrics (horsepower, mileage, engine displacement, transmission) and currency displays to eliminate vertical scanning friction.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Uppercase transformations are used selectively on `label-sm` for vehicle badge classifications (`NUEVO`, `SEMINUEVO`) with widened letter spacing (`0.04em`) to ensure legibility at micro dimensions.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored on a standard 8pt spatial grid configured into a 12-column responsive system.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Grids
- **Desktop (1200px and up):** 12-column grid, `margin: 3rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Vehicle listing grids render in 3 columns for balanced card proportion and image dominance.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column grid, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.25rem`. Vehicle listings adjust to a 2-column format; filter drawers transition to collapsible side-sheets.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px):** 4-column grid, `margin: 1.25rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Vehicle listing cards shift to a single full-width vertical stack. Key controls remain pinned in sticky bottom sheets for one-handed thumb interaction.

### Vertical Rhythm
Section blocks adhere to generous vertical spacing (`space-xl` scaled dynamically up to `4rem` for hero transitions) to preserve the breathing room characteristic of physical luxury showrooms. Component interiors utilize tight, controlled intervals (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) to group related mechanical specifications seamlessly.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth combines crisp surface separation, whisper-thin borders, and deep ambient drop shadows inspired by precision glass and studio vehicle lighting.

### Tonal Hierarchy & Shadows
- **Base Level (Canvas):** Flat `#F8FAFC` slate background.
- **Level 1 (Listing Cards, Form Panels):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface bordered by an ultra-thin low-contrast line (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`). Shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 6px 16px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover States, Floating Filter Bars):** Elevated via `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Vehicle Quick-View Drawers, WhatsApp Floating Triggers):** High-ambient lift: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.16)`.

Glassmorphic treatments (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` background) are reserved exclusively for sticky filter navigation headers and image gallery counter pills over vehicle imagery.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a balanced roundedness architecture (`roundedness: 2` / base `0.5rem` / `8px`):

- **Cards & Media Containers:** `1rem` (`rounded-lg`) ensures vehicle photography feels refined and frame-mounted without clipping essential wheel or bumper proportions.
- **Interactive Controls (Inputs, Dropdowns):** `0.5rem` (`rounded`) maintains structure and functional clarity.
- **Badges, Filter Pills, and Floating Concierge Actions:** Complete pill-shape (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to create an immediate visual contrast against geometric card corners and rigid spec grids.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep Obsidian background (`#0F172A`), white text, `0.5rem` radius, bold weight. On hover: shifts to `#1E293B` with subtle scale lift.
- **Secondary (Transaction / Reserve):** Rich Amber (`#D97706`), white text, elevated shadow on interaction.
- **WhatsApp Concierge Button:** Dedicated `#25D366` surface with crisp white iconography and bold typography. In desktop headers, it renders as a pill button with a live pulse indicator; on mobile, it docks to the lower right corner as an anchored action pill (`padding: 0.75rem 1.25rem`).
- **Ghost / Tertiary:** Borderless `#0F172A` text with subtle slate hover tint (`#F1F5F9`).

### Vehicle Status Badges
Rendered as pill-shaped chips with strict color pairs and upper-case typography (`label-sm`):
- **Nuevo:** Text `#059669`, Background `#ECFDF5`, Border `#A7F3D0`.
- **Seminuevo:** Text `#0891B2`, Background `#ECFEFF`, Border `#A5F3FC`.
- **Reservado:** Text `#D97706`, Background `#FFFBEB`, Border `#FDE68A`.
- **Vendido:** Text `#64748B`, Background `#F1F5F9`, Border `#E2E8F0`. Opacity reduced across vehicle card.
- **Próximamente:** Text `#7C3AED`, Background `#F5F3FF`, Border `#DDD6FE`.

### Automotive Cards
- **Image Zone:** 16:10 aspect ratio with an overlay container holding the Status Badge at top-left, and Favorite heart trigger at top-right. Bottom-left houses a gallery image count pill (`1/18`) with glassmorphism.
- **Body Zone:** Brand & model hierarchy (`headline-sm`), followed by year and trim subtitle (`body-sm`).
- **Spec Ribbon:** A 3-column micro-grid displaying Fuel/Transmission, Mileage (km), and Engine/Horsepower accompanied by minimal monochrome iconography.
- **Footer Zone:** Emphasized price block with monthly financing estimate underneath, accompanied by direct WhatsApp or "Ver Detalles" action triggers.

### Filter Chips & Tags
Pill-shaped selectors (`padding: 0.5rem 1rem`).
- **Default State:** White background, `#E2E8F0` border, `#0F172A` label.
- **Active State:** Deep Obsidian background (`#0F172A`), white label, `#0F172A` border with a smooth checkmark icon or counter badge.
- **EV / Hybrid Tag:** Subtly accented with Cyan border (`#06B6D4`) when enabled.

### Spec Badges
Compact data modules used on Vehicle Detail Pages (VDP). Designed as soft tiles (`0.5rem` radius, `#F8FAFC` background, subtle `#E2E8F0` border) pairing a top micro-label (e.g., `POTENCIA`) with high-contrast tabular values (e.g., `380 CV`).

### Form Inputs & Selectors
White background, `0.5rem` border radius, `#CBD5E1` stroke. Focused state introduces an Obsidian stroke (`#0F172A`) paired with a `3px` focus ring tinted at `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1)`. Placeholder text uses `#94A3B8`.
